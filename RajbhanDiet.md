## 📌 **Workout: 8:00 AM**

📌 **Target Macros:**

- **Calories:** ~2000 kcal
- **Protein:** **115g**
- **Carbs:** **200-220g**
- **Fats:** **70-75g**

---

### **🛏️ Early Morning (6:30 - 7:00 AM) → Pre-Workout Meal**

- **1 banana OR ½ cup oats with water/milk**
- **1 tsp peanut butter**

📊 **Calories:** 250 | **Protein:** 6g | **Carbs:** 40g | **Fats:** 5g

💡 *Provides quick energy for your workout!*

---

### **🏋️ Post-Workout (9:15 AM) → Recovery Meal**

- **4 boiled egg whites OR 80g paneer**
- **½ apple OR ½ cup pomegranate**

📊 **Calories:** 220 | **Protein:** 25g | **Carbs:** 20g | **Fats:** 3g

💡 *Supports muscle recovery without excess protein intake!*

---

### **🍳 Breakfast (10:30 AM)**

- **1 roti OR 1 multigrain toast**
- **Scrambled eggs (2 whole + 2 whites) OR Paneer bhurji (80g)**
- **1 cup green tea/black coffee**

📊 **Calories:** 400 | **Protein:** 28g | **Carbs:** 45g | **Fats:** 12g

💡 *Balanced meal with sustained energy & muscle support!*

---

### **🍛 Lunch (2:00 PM)**

- **1.5 roti OR 1 cup cooked brown rice**
- **1 bowl dal (moong/chana/masoor) OR 80g paneer sabzi**
- **1 cup curd (plain, no sugar)**
- **1 cucumber + tomato salad**

📊 **Calories:** 500 | **Protein:** 30g | **Carbs:** 65g | **Fats:** 12g

💡 *High-fiber meal to keep you full & provide energy!*

---

### **🍏 Evening Snack (5:00 PM)**

- **Green tea/black coffee (no sugar)**
- **Roasted chana OR 1 boiled egg with black salt**
- **Handful of nuts (almonds/walnuts)**

📊 **Calories:** 250 | **Protein:** 10g | **Carbs:** 25g | **Fats:** 12g

💡 *Healthy fats & light protein to keep cravings away!*

---

### **🥘 Dinner (8:00 PM)**

- **1 large bowl vegetable soup (broccoli, carrot, spinach, tomato, etc.)**
- **1 roti OR ½ cup cooked quinoa**
- **80g paneer/tofu OR 2 boiled eggs**

📊 **Calories:** 400 | **Protein:** 25g | **Carbs:** 50g | **Fats:** 12g

💡 *A lighter but protein-rich meal for fat loss!*

---

### **🌙 Before Bed (10:30 PM) → Slow-Digesting Protein**

- **1 glass warm milk (low-fat) + ½ tsp turmeric**
- **5 soaked almonds OR 1 tsp peanut butter**

📊 **Calories:** 150 | **Protein:** 10g | **Carbs:** 10g | **Fats:** 6g

💡 *Supports muscle recovery overnight!*

---

### **📊 FINAL DAILY MACROS:**

- **Calories:** ~2000 kcal
- **Protein:** **115g** 💪
- **Carbs:** **200-220g**
- **Fats:** **70-75g**
